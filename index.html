The Campaign for Nuclear Disarmament (CND) in Lincoln has been part of a wider movement that believes the world would be safer without nuclear weapons. Linked to the national CND, the local group focused on raising awareness about the dangers of nuclear arms and promoting peaceful alternatives. Its work was based on the idea that security should come from cooperation and diplomacy rather than weapons of mass destruction.
